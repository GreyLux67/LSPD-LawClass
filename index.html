Lesson 1 - Constitutional Law

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the U.S. Constitution.
  • Understand why officers must follow constitutional law.
  • Learn the 1st, 4th, 5th, 6th, and 14th Amendments.

1st Amendment

Protects freedom of speech, religion, press, assembly, and petition. Police cannot arrest someone simply because they criticize law enforcement.

4th Amendment

Protects people from unreasonable searches and seizures. Officers generally need probable cause or another legal exception before searching someone.

5th Amendment

Protects against self-incrimination. This is one reason Miranda warnings exist.

Scenario

A person records you during a traffic stop while standing on the sidewalk. Should you arrest them? No. Recording police in public is generally protected by the First Amendment as long as they are not interfering.
